🎨 Auto Draw – Tutorial

Follow these simple steps to start drawing automatically.

1. <b> Generate Your Image </b>

Upload any image to ChatGPT and use this prompt to convert it into a high-contrast grayscale comic-book illustration.

Image Generation Prompt

Transform the uploaded reference image into a high-contrast cinematic black-and-white comic-book vector illustration with vibrant red neon accents.

People and composition:

- Preserve all people's original faces, facial features, hairstyles, expressions, body proportions, clothing, and exact positions.
- Do not add, remove, or replace anyone.
- Maintain the original birthday group composition.

Art style:

- Pure monochrome black, white, and multiple shades of grey for all people.
- Sharp cel-shading with clearly separated grey tonal layers.
- Deep black shadows and bright white highlights.
- Clean, precise vector outlines.
- Dramatic comic-book illustration aesthetic.
- No skin-tone colours, no peach, no brown, no warm colour tint.

Red accent lighting:

- Vibrant neon red highlights around hair, shoulders, clothing edges and selected contours.
- Red glowing reflections on decorative elements.
- Keep red visually striking against the monochrome subjects.

Background and decorations:

- Pure clean white background.
- Preserve the birthday decorations, balloons, ribbons, birthday lettering, cake, candles and table arrangement.
- Use glossy black and vibrant red balloons.
- Birthday lettering should be black with subtle red neon outlines.
- Preserve the original arrangement and proportions.

Lighting and finish:

- High contrast.
- Dramatic directional lighting.
- Deep blacks with crisp white highlights.
- Professional digital vector illustration.
- Extremely clean edges and detailed cel-shading.

STRICT RULES:

1. Do not change anyone's identity or facial appearance.
2. Do not change the original composition.
3. No peach, beige, brown or natural skin colours.
4. People must remain strictly black, white and grey.
5. Red must remain visibly present as an accent colour.
6. Background must remain pure white.

Create a polished, high-resolution 4K illustration.

2. Upload Image

- Save the generated image.
- Open your phone Gallery.
- Make sure the image is available.

3. Enable Permissions

Before drawing, enable these permissions:

- Accessibility Permission
- Display Over Other Apps

Both permissions are required.

4. Open Drawing Controls

- Open Auto Draw.
- Click Open Drawing Control.

5. Adjust Canvas

- Set the canvas size.
- Align it with your reference image.

6. Select Colour

- Choose the required drawing colour.
- Match it with your reference image.

7. Start Drawing!

Everything is ready. Click Start Drawing and enjoy! ❤️
